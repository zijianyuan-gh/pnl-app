Daybook — Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026 · Applies to the Daybook iOS app, the Daybook web app and the Daybook Mac app.

Summary

Daybook does not collect any data. Everything you enter — accounts, trades, transfers, notes — is stored only on your own device. There is no sign-up, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer cannot see your data.

What Daybook stores, and where

  • Your records are kept in the app's local database on your device.
  • iOS app: after every change the app also saves an automatic backup file in its own folder (Files → On My iPhone → Daybook). Like other app data, it is included in your iPhone backups (iCloud Backup or your computer) if you use them.
  • Backup and CSV files you export go only where you choose to save or send them (for example iCloud Drive). These files are not encrypted by Daybook; keep them somewhere private.
  • App Lock (optional): Face ID / Touch ID is handled entirely by iOS. Daybook only receives "unlocked" or "not unlocked" and never has access to face or fingerprint data. App Lock is a screen lock; it does not add encryption to your data.

What Daybook does not do

  • It does not send your data anywhere. The iOS app makes no network requests; its content is built into the app.
  • It does not use third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking.
  • It does not connect to brokers or exchanges, execute trades, hold money or give investment advice.

Web version

The web version is served by GitHub Pages. When your browser downloads the app's files, GitHub may process technical information such as your IP address, as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement. Your records still stay only in your browser on your device.

Deleting your data

Use Accounts → Erase all data in the app, or delete the app. In the iOS app, deleting the app also deletes its automatic backup files; backup files you exported yourself must be deleted where you saved them.

Children

Daybook is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub or see the support page.

Daybook 隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 11 月 12 日 · 适用于 Daybook iOS App、网页版和 Mac 版。

概述

Daybook 不收集任何数据。你录入的账户、交易、资金记录和备注只保存在你自己的设备上。无需注册，没有服务器，没有统计分析、广告或追踪，开发者无法看到你的数据。

保存的内容与位置

  • 你的记录保存在设备上的本地数据库中。
  • iOS App：每次修改后还会在 App 自己的文件夹（文件 → 我的 iPhone → Daybook）保存一份自动备份。与其他 App 数据一样，若你使用 iCloud 云备份或电脑备份，它会包含在 iPhone 备份中。
  • 你导出的备份或 CSV 文件只会保存到你选择的位置（如 iCloud 云盘）。Daybook 不对这些文件加密，请妥善保管。
  • 应用锁（可选）：面容 ID / 触控 ID 完全由 iOS 处理，Daybook 只会得到"已解锁/未解锁"的结果，无法访问面容或指纹数据。应用锁是屏幕锁，不会对数据另行加密。

Daybook 不会做的事

  • 不会把你的数据发送到任何地方。iOS App 不发起任何网络请求，其内容内置在 App 中。
  • 不使用任何第三方统计、广告、崩溃上报或追踪 SDK。
  • 不连接券商或交易所，不执行交易，不托管资金，不提供投资建议。

网页版

网页版由 GitHub Pages 托管。浏览器下载 App 文件时，GitHub 可能按照 GitHub 隐私声明 处理 IP 地址等技术信息。你的记录仍只保存在你设备上的浏览器中。

删除数据

在 App 中使用「账户 → 清空全部数据」，或删除 App。删除 iOS App 会同时删除其自动备份文件；你自行导出的备份需在保存位置自行删除。

儿童

Daybook 并非面向儿童，也不收集任何人的数据。

变更与联系

本政策如有变更，会在本页面发布新版本并更新生效日期。如有问题，请 在 GitHub 提交 Issue 或查看 支持页面。
