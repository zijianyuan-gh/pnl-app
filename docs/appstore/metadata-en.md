**Description:**

> Daybook is a calendar journal for your own trading results. Log each day's realized profit or loss in seconds and see your month at a glance — green days, red days, and exactly how much.
>
> RECORD IN SECONDS
> • Pick an account, choose Profit or Loss, type the amount, save.
> • Optional details: symbol, platform, strategy tags, fees, notes, side, entry/exit prices and times.
> • Amounts are exact to the cent; profit and loss are always shown with a + or − sign, never by color alone.
>
> SEE YOUR MONTH
> • A full calendar with each day's net P&L and number of trades.
> • Month P&L and an estimated balance per account (starting balance + deposits − withdrawals + realized P&L).
>
> UNDERSTAND YOUR TRADING
> • Trade win rate and green-day rate, clearly defined and kept separate.
> • Average win and loss, profit factor, best and worst days, winning and losing streaks.
> • Cumulative P&L curve, monthly bars and results by strategy tag.
>
> MULTIPLE ACCOUNTS
> • Keep stocks, futures, options or prediction-market accounts separate, or view them together.
> • Deposits and withdrawals are tracked separately and never counted as profit.
>
> YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
> • No sign-up, no server, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Daybook collects no data.
> • Works fully offline — everything is stored on your iPhone.
> • Automatic backup copy in Files, plus full JSON backups and CSV export you can save to iCloud Drive.
> • Import CSV files from your spreadsheet or broker export.
> • Optional App Lock with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode.
>
> Daybook is a personal record-keeping and analysis tool. It does not connect to brokers or exchanges, execute trades, hold money or give investment advice. Results you enter are your own records.
